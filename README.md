# Pencil & Pen

**AI holds the pencil. You hold the pen.**

Our Tech Corps Hackathon project. We believe AI is a powerful tool for finding information and brainstorming ideas, but it should never dictate your decisions. The final call, and the responsibility that comes with it, stays with people.

## What's here

- `index.html`: the interactive site. It's one file with no build step. Open it in any browser.
- `SCRIPT.md`: the 5-minute pitch script for all five speakers, with a rubric checklist and Q&A prep.

## Sections of the site

1. **Hero:** type your own answer and the AI's pencil suggestion gets crossed out.
2. **What we believe:** the core belief and three supporting ideas.
3. **How AI works:** a next-word prediction demo, plus real cases where AI got it wrong.
4. **The Lab:** a guided assignment where the AI drafts and you choose, check, question, and decide. One of the AI's facts is fake.
5. **Human jobs:** Check, Question, Decide, our line, and a "Pencil or pen?" sorting activity.
6. **Our voices:** team stories and a pledge board.
7. **Why it looks like this:** what each design choice means.

## Personalizing

Edit `TEAM_NAME`, `TEAM`, and `TEAM_PLEDGES` at the top of the `<script>` in `index.html`. Use your real stories.
