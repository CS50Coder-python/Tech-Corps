What we believe about AI

AI is a powerful tool for finding information and brainstorming ideas, but it should never make your decisions for you. The final call, and the responsibility for it, belongs to people.

the real-life AI we mean

Students asking chatbots for help

ChatGPT, Gemini, Snapchat's My AI. Most of us have used at least one for homework, and some of our friends ask them for advice about actual life stuff. So what is the chatbot doing when it answers you?

26%

of U.S. teens said they used ChatGPT for schoolwork in 2024. That's double the 13% from the year before. (Pew Research Center, January 2025)

How a chatbot picks its answer

It writes one word at a time. For each word, it ranks what's most likely to come next, based on patterns in tons of text that other people wrote. Pick a question to see it happen.

We simplified this. The percentages are made up to show the idea, but ranking words by how likely they are is really how these models work.

When the guess is wrong

Since it's predicting what sounds right, it can say things that sound confident and are totally false. People call this a hallucination.

2023

Two New York lawyers were fined $5,000 for turning in a court filing with six cases ChatGPT made up. The cases sounded real, and nobody checked. (Mata v. Avianca)

2023

Google's own ad for its Bard chatbot said the James Webb Space Telescope took the first picture of a planet outside our solar system. It didn't, and the mistake was in Google's own promo.

every day

Recommendation AI, like TikTok's For You page, predicts what will keep you watching and picks your next video. You never chose what it shows you, but it shapes what feels normal.

Why this matters to us

If the AI is wrong and you don't check, its mistake becomes your mistake. Your name is on the essay, not the chatbot's. And when it picks for you, it picks what's popular, which might not be what's right for you.

where people stay involved

Three jobs AI doesn't get

Using AI is fine. These are the parts we think a person always has to do, and why.

Check

Look up anything before you use it.

Why: AI sounds just as sure when it's wrong. You're the one who gets graded, fined, or embarrassed.

Question

Ask who disagrees, what's missing, and how it knows.

Why: AI leans toward the most common answer, so the people it leaves out are easy to miss.

Decide

Make the final call yourself and own it.

Why: decisions affect real people. A program can't take responsibility for anything. You can.

our line

The more a decision affects a person, like their grades, health, friendships, or future, the more a human needs to be the one holding the pen.

our creative choices

How we made it

We changed our minds a lot while building this. Here are the biggest decisions and what each one says about our belief.

Plug in a real chatbot Write the AI's lines ourselves

A real AI could make up facts at random, and we didn't want our own site to fool anyone. So we wrote every AI line ourselves and put in one fake fact on purpose so you could catch it.

Cut the "Pick for me" button Keep it, but make it refuse

Half of us wanted to delete it since it doesn't do anything. We kept it because watching the AI hand the choice back makes our point faster than a paragraph would.

Gray for AI, blue for people AI writes in pencil, you write in pen

Just using colors didn't really mean anything. Once someone said "AI writes in pencil," everything clicked, and every page on the site follows that rule now.
