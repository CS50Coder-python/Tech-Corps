The real-life AI we're talking about

Students asking chatbots for help

ChatGPT, Gemini, Snapchat's My AI. Teens use them for homework, and more and more for advice on real choices. So what is the chatbot actually doing when it answers?

26% of U.S. teens said they'd used ChatGPT for schoolwork in 2024, double the 13% from 2023. (Pew Research Center, January 2025)

How a chatbot picks its answer

A chatbot writes one word at a time. Each time, it ranks which word is most likely to come next, based on patterns in huge amounts of text written by other people. Pick a question to see it.

This is a simplified picture. The percentages are made up to show the idea, but the ranking-by-likelihood part is how these models really work.

When the guess goes wrong

Because it's predicting what sounds right, it can produce things that are confident and false. People call this a hallucination.

2023

Two New York lawyers were fined $5,000 after they turned in a court filing with six cases that ChatGPT made up. The cases sounded real. Nobody checked. (Mata v. Avianca)

2023

In Google's own launch ad for its Bard chatbot, it said the James Webb Space Telescope took the first picture of a planet outside our solar system. That was wrong, and it ran in the company's own promo.

Every day

Recommendation AI, like TikTok's For You page, predicts what will keep you watching and picks your next video. It's influencing what you see, and what feels normal, without you choosing.

!

Why it matters to us: if the AI is wrong and you don't check, its mistake becomes your mistake. Your name goes on the essay, not the chatbot's. And if it's picking for you, it's picking what's popular, not what's right for you.

Human responsibility

Three jobs AI doesn't get to take

Using AI is fine. These are the spots where a person has to stay in the loop, and the reason for each.

Check

Look up anything you're going to use before you use it.

Why: AI sounds just as sure when it's wrong. You're the one who gets graded, fined, or embarrassed.

Question

Ask who disagrees, what's missing, and how it knows.

Why: AI leans toward the most common answer. The people it leaves out are often the ones who matter most.

Decide

Make the final call yourself and own it.

Why: decisions land on real people. AI can't be responsible for anything. You can.

Our line

The more a decision affects a person, like their grades, health, relationships, or future, the more a human has to be the one holding the pen.

Creative choices

Why it looks like this

Every design choice on this site carries our belief. Here's what each one means.

AI text

Gray, dashed, typewriter font = AI

Everything the AI says looks like pencil. It's a draft. You can erase it.

your call

Blue handwriting = you

Only human decisions get ink. When you choose something, it changes from pencil to pen right in front of you.

checked

Red pen = checking

Teachers use red pen to catch mistakes. On this site, you're the one holding it.

pick for me

"Pick for me" never works

We put that button in the Lab on purpose. Press it and the AI hands the choice back to you.

waiting for you

The app waits for you

You can't move forward in the Lab until you make a choice. Nothing gets decided automatically.
