# Pencil & Pen: 5-Minute Script (easy to memorize)

*[Brackets]* are actions. Don't say them.

---

### ME (0:00–0:45) · Hook and belief

Raise your hand if you've used AI for homework this year. *[pause]*

Same. All five of us use it, so we're not here to tell you to stop.

But in 2023, two lawyers used ChatGPT to find court cases. The cases were fake. Nobody checked, and they got fined five thousand dollars.

So here's what we believe: **AI is great for finding information and brainstorming ideas, but it should never make your decisions for you.**

We say it like this: **AI holds the pencil. You hold the pen.**

Pencil is a draft. Pen is what you sign.

### ELIJAH (0:45–1:45) · Three reasons and how AI works

We have three reasons.

One: AI is a great starting point.
Two: it's guessing.
Three: if you never decide, you get worse at deciding.

*[Scroll to "How AI works." Click "College major."]*

Here's what guessing means. A chatbot like ChatGPT picks the word most likely to come next, based on what other people wrote.

So it says my best major is Computer Science. It doesn't know me. That's just the most common answer.

*[Click the "Tell it" button.]*

If I tell it more, it gets closer. But it's still guessing.

And if it guesses wrong and you don't check, its mistake becomes your mistake.

### ME (1:45–3:00) · Live demo

*[Scroll to "The Lab." Click "Ask the AI for ideas."]*

So we built this. The AI's ideas show up in gray pencil because they're just drafts.

*[Click "Just pick for me."]*

If I ask it to pick for me, it says no. That's on purpose.

*[Close it. Pick a topic.]*

When I choose, it turns into blue pen. Now it's my decision.

*[Click Next. Check all three facts.]*

It gives me three facts. Two are real. This one is fake. AI made it up. That's called a hallucination, so I toss it.

*[Toss it, keep the others, click Next, ask one question.]*

Then I ask what it left out.

*[Click Next, type one sentence, click "Write it in pen."]*

Then I write my own answer. Stamped: decided by a human.

AI saved me time. I made the decisions.

### MEMBER 3 (3:00–3:40) · Human jobs

*[Scroll to "Your jobs."]*

People have three jobs AI can't take.

**Check.** AI sounds sure even when it's wrong.
**Question.** AI gives the most common answer, not the whole story.
**Decide.** AI can't take responsibility. You can.

Our rule: the more a decision affects a person, the more a human should make it.

Like cheating. AI detectors have blamed students who didn't cheat. A teacher should decide that, not a program.

### MEMBER 4 (3:40–4:10) · Our stories

*[Scroll to "Our stories."]*

This comes from our own lives.

*(Say your real story in one or two sentences.)*

We're the first students with AI in every class. We hope schools teach us how to use it, not just ban it.

### MEMBER 5 (4:10–4:40) · How we made it

*[Scroll to "How we made it."]*

Every design choice on our site has a meaning.

Gray pencil is the AI, because it's a draft.
Blue pen is you, because your choice counts.
Red is for checking.

We almost used a real chatbot. But it could make up facts and fool people. So we wrote the AI's lines ourselves, with one fake fact on purpose.

### ME (4:40–5:00) · Close

*[Scroll to the bottom.]*

So use AI. Let it search. Let it brainstorm.

But when it's time to decide, remember:

**AI holds the pencil. You hold the pen.**

Thank you.
